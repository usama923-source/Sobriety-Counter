Privacy Policy

QuitDrink Ai: Sober Tracker · Effective date: October 17, 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains, in plain language, what QuitDrink Ai ("we", "the app") does with your information. It is written to match what the app actually does — nothing more.

1. No account required

QuitDrink Ai does not require you to create an account and asks for no name, email address, or phone number. Almost everything you enter stays on your device. The only feature that uses an internet connection is the optional AI Friend chat; all other core features work offline. Every feature in the app is free to use.

2. Information stored on your device

The following information is saved locally in the app's private storage on your device. We do not upload it to our own servers:

  • Your quit (sobriety) date and the live counter derived from it.
  • Daily check-ins — the days you marked as alcohol-free or not.
  • Your saved personal reasons for quitting.
  • Journal entries you write.
  • Weight tracker entries (goal weight, current weight, and log).
  • Your AI Friend chat history, kept on the device (last 50 messages).
  • Favourite quotes, savings settings, currency, theme, and the daily reminder time.
  • Breathing-session history and best time.

This data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. On Android, your device may also back up this app data to your Google account if Android backup is enabled in your device settings.

3. A random device ID

On first launch, the app generates a random, anonymous device ID (a UUID) and stores it on your device. It is not your name, email, or phone number, and it is not linked to any account. It is used only to keep basic app usage consistent across sessions.

4. Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics (a Google service) to understand how the app is used in aggregate. The app records simple events such as:

  • when the app is opened;
  • when a daily reminder is scheduled.

These events do not include your name, email, contact details, the content of your journal or reasons, your weight entries, or your chat messages. Firebase Analytics may also collect standard technical information such as an app-instance identifier and general device and usage information, as described in Google's own privacy policy. This data is not used to show you advertising inside the app.

5. AI Friend chat

The AI Friend chat is optional. When you send a message and the AI service is configured, your messages — together with the recent conversation history needed to generate a reply — are sent to the Groq API (groq.com) to produce a response. Groq's own privacy policy applies to those transmissions.

We do not store your chat messages on our own servers. Your chat history is saved on your device, and you can clear it at any time with the delete button in the chat screen. If the app has no internet connection or no AI key is configured, it responds with built-in offline replies and no message is sent anywhere.

AI chat has a daily message limit. The number of messages you have sent today is counted on your device to enforce that limit, and that count never leaves your device.

6. Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled and shown locally on your device. We do not send push notifications from a server, and enabling reminders does not send your personal data to us. You can turn the reminder off at any time in Settings or in your device settings.

7. We do not sell your data

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share information only with the service providers named above (Google Firebase for analytics and Groq for AI replies) to the extent needed to operate the app.

8. Deleting your data

You are in control of your data and there are two simple ways to remove it:

  • In the app: open Settings → Reset All Data. This erases your sobriety date, check-ins, reasons, journal entries, weight entries, chat history, and preferences stored on the device, and cancels scheduled reminders.
  • Uninstall the app: removing the app deletes the data stored in its private storage on your device.

Aggregate analytics already recorded by Google Firebase is not removed by uninstalling the app. If you would like this analytics data deleted, contact us using the email address below.

9. Children

QuitDrink Ai is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Not medical advice

QuitDrink Ai is a motivational and self-tracking tool. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for professional care, diagnosis, or treatment. The AI Friend is not a therapist or medical professional.

Stopping heavy drinking suddenly can be dangerous. Please talk to a doctor before making big changes, especially if you drink heavily or have withdrawal symptoms.

If you are in crisis or in an emergency, call your local emergency number right away, or call 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline in the United States. Help is available, and you do not have to face this alone.
